Quick rules

How to play

  • Play a card matching the active color, or play Wild.
  • Number cards give that many shovel/flag actions.
  • Shovel once to reveal; double-click or choose Flag to mark a mine.
  • Reveal a mine and the other player wins immediately.
  • Safe reveals are worth 1 point; flags are worth 2, with penalties for wrong flags.
  • Draw cards are random and drawing ends the turn.
